# Billing requirements

Written in a team's own format: one requirement per line, `REQ: <id> - <text>`.

## Invoices

REQ: BILL-1 - When an order is paid, the billing service shall issue an invoice.
REQ: BILL-2 - The billing service shall number invoices without gaps.

## Reminders

REQ: BILL-3 - If an invoice is unpaid after 30 days, then the billing service shall send a reminder.
REQ: bill-4 - lower-case ids are not spec ids and are skipped.
